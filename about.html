I'm Giada, an Italian-Spanish graphic designer based in Barcelona. Growing up between two cultures has shaped the way I see design, making me naturally curious, adaptable, and attentive to different ways of communicating.

I'm interested in building visual identities that balance clarity, typography, and narrative, whether for books, brands, or cultural projects.

Services

1. Editorial Design

Designing books, magazines, and publications with a strong focus on typography, hierarchy, and narrative.

2. Brand Design

Creating visual identities that are clear, consistent, and built to last—from logos and typography to complete design systems.

3. Art Direction

Developing visual concepts and creative systems that give projects a coherent and recognizable direction.

4. Visual Comunication

Transforming ideas into clear, engaging visuals across print and digital media.
